The People

Meet the Band

Ira and the Half Moon is a five-piece live band from the Pacific Northwest with a catalog built around songs you already know and a show built to make you feel it. Rock, country, alt-rock, and acoustic grit — sometimes all in the same set. We play loud enough to get the room moving and tight enough that you'd notice if we didn't.

Brandon Ard
Lead Vocals · Frontman

The voice and face of the operation. Brandon brings the energy, holds the stage, and somehow remembers the words to every song — which is more impressive than it sounds at 11pm on a Saturday.

Maddy
Vocals

Maddy's voice adds depth, range, and the kind of harmonies that make people stop their conversation mid-sentence to listen. The band's secret weapon, and not so secret anymore.

Dylan Thoma
Drums · Musical Director

Dylan keeps the band honest and on time — both musically and logistically. As Musical Director, he's the one who decides whether something sounds ready, and he's usually right.

Tim Roland
Guitar

Tim handles the guitar work with enough range to cover clean country leads one song and full-drive rock the next. Reliable, tasteful, and the kind of player who makes everyone around him sound better.

Tom Mahoney
Bass

Tom is the foundation — the low-end that keeps the whole thing locked and moving. He doesn't need a lot of words. The bass does the talking.
